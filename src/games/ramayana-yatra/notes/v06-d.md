## The choice
One `ask` step with **one option**, "Take the whole mountain!" (one tap, or key 1). It cannot fail: the clock waits on it,
the only path is the lift, and a skip or `advance` (no answer) plays/ends the same path (`toldPath(c, []) = toldPath(c, [0])`).
The alternative "look for the herbs, they hide, the same choice again" needs a question inside an option, which WP0's
engine refuses (`checkTold`: a question sits at the top level only); the hiding is shown before the question instead.
No `set` flag (nothing reads it). `seen.herbs` is set when the scene ends (played or skipped).

  नयतु, करिष्यति, गृहीत्वा, उत्तिष्ठन्ति, निद्रायाः … (oracle levels in the test).
- Option phrasing: सर्वं पर्वतम् एव नयतु! — 3rd-person imperative (answers "What will Hanumān do?", polite to Hanumān as the
  sambhashana rules ask for heroes); नी "take" for "take".
- For rev1 / rev2: रामे … दया (locative of the object of compassion); ओषधयः तम् आगतं जानन्ति (जानाति + acc. + predicative kta);
  निद्रायाः इव उत्तिष्ठन्ति (ablative "from sleep").

## 3D (cutscene3d-herbs.ts)
- Same engine approach as the Āditya scene: raw WebGL 1, the view sizes the canvas (≤ 420 px wide internally), nearest
  sampling, dithered colour bands; the live battle frame is a plane that folds back (rotate-out) and stands up again
  (rotate-in) — `framePlane` / `FRAME_POSE` reused; the camera arcs from the frame to the first shot and back (`herbsPath`).
- Two sets: the battlefield at night (the fallen army as lying billboards that rise; the sea with the peak's glitter; Laṅkā on
  Trikūṭa) and the Himālaya over a sea of clouds (golden Ṛṣabha, white Kailāsa with strata, the herb mountain as flat-shaded
  meshes; the far range; twelve herb lights in the four colours). The herb mountain's peak is its own mesh with caps: torn,
  raised over Hanumān's head, carried, shining (emissive + halo, the sky and sea lit from it).
- One shot per `{ "scene" }` step (cuts); choreography on the shot clock; ambient motion on its own clock (it keeps moving
  while the question waits — the scene clock stops there). Skip from the Himālaya: the rotate-in starts from the revive pose
  over the battlefield (the frame lives there).
- Budget: ~17 draw calls (sprites and lights batched per texture), < 3 000 triangles, one 16×16 light texture, the game's
  sprite strips. Context lost → the view falls back to the 2D painter (WP0); restored → 3D again.
- **Exports added to cutscene3d.ts** (no other change): `add sub scale dot cross norm lerp3 perspective lookAt mul spriteStrip
  groundCanvas cloudCanvas dotCanvas VS_TEX FS_TEX VS_SKY DITHER FS_SKY VS_SEA FS_SEA Prog hexRgb` (+ a doc paragraph).
  cutscene.test (15) and the V05B_E browser tests (4 + the RY_SHOTS case skipped) green after it.

## 2D (cutscene-paint-d.ts)
A still per shot with `cue.reduced` (the view's 2D mode); without it (no WebGL / lost context) the same pictures move a
little. Layers are cached per shot (painted once). The engine adds the flash / shake kit effects in 2D.
