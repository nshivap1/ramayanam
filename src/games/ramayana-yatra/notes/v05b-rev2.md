1. **P1 — `verses[0].en` (6.105.1): wrong agent.**
   - Before: "Then Rāma stood on the battlefield, worn out by the fighting and deep in thought, and saw Rāvaṇa before him, ready to fight again."
   - After: "Then the sage saw Rāma standing on the battlefield, worn out by the fighting and deep in thought, and Rāvaṇa before him, ready to fight."
   - Why:
     - Verses 1–2 are one sentence. युद्धपरिश्रान्तं … स्थितम् are accusatives that agree with रामम् (v2), and दृष्ट्वा shares the agent of अब्रवीत्, who is Agastya (3.4.21 समानकर्तृकयोः पूर्वकाले). So the one who sees Rāma and Rāvaṇa is Agastya, not Rāma.
     - valmikiramayan.net's own translation agrees: "…and seeing Ravana … facing him duly prepared for the battle, the glorious sage, Agastya … spoke".
     - "again" is not in the verse.
   - verses[1].en ("The holy sage Agastya … went up to Rāma and said:") can stay.
