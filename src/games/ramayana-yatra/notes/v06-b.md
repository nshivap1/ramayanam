- **4.5.11 मे / वा → वा.** valmikiramayan.net reads यदि मे (Devanagari page and its transliteration page). The IIT
  Kanpur vulgate text (4.5.12) and the critical edition (4.5.12) both read यदि वा. Two independent witnesses against
  one site, so वा is stored. Also बध्यतां (site, IIT Kanpur) vs वध्यतां (CE, b/v spelling): बध्यतां stored.

  Kanpur text read the accusative (the arrow pierced the sālas and the mountain slope). The CE reads the locative
  गिरिप्रस्थे (the sālas on the mountain slope). The vulgate reading is stored.
- **4.28.7:** the CE e-text (enjoylearningsanskrit and indology.info alike) prints धर्मपरिक्लिष्टा. Two vulgate
  witnesses print घर्म- ("summer heat"), which the sense requires. Treated as an e-text slip; pādas follow IIT Kanpur.

## The deer show the way: Araṇya sarga 64 (re-fetched 14 Nov; text as the site prints it, words split)
**Important: the deer passage is NOT in the critical edition.** The CE 3.60 (= vulgate 3.64) goes from 3.60.14
(godāvarīṃ janasthānam imaṃ prasravaṇaṃ girim / sarvāṇy anucariṣyāmi yadi sītā hi dṛśyate = vulgate 3.64.14cd–15ab)
straight to 3.60.15 (evaṃ saṃbhāṣamāṇau tāv anyonyaṃ bhrātarāv ubhau / vasuṃdharāyāṃ patitaṃ puṣpamārgam apaśyatām =
vulgate 3.64.24cd–25ab), checked line by line in the indology.info e-text. So vulgate 3.64.15cd–24ab (the deer) are
vulgate (Southern recension) only. **The Godāvarī's silence is in the CE** (3.60.6c–9).

The river is silent (vulgate 3.64.7–9; CE 3.60.7–9):
- 3.64.7 भूतानि राक्षसेन्द्रेण वध अर्हेण हृताम् अपि | न ताम् शशंसू रामाय तथा गोदावरी नदी || (CE 3.60.7: same words:
  bhūtāni rākṣasendreṇa vadhārheṇa hṛtām api / na tāṃ śaśaṃsū rāmāya tathā godāvarī nadī). The beings and the
  Godāvarī did not tell Rāma about Sītā, although Rāvaṇa, who deserved death, had carried her off. Meaning: "the
  beings, and the river Godāvarī too, did not tell Rāma of her". IIT Kanpur prints हृतामिति … नदि.
- 3.64.8 ततः प्रचोदिता भूतैः शंस च अस्मै प्रियाम् इति | न च सा हि अवदत् सीताम् पृष्टा रामेण शोचता || (CE 3.60.8:
  tataḥ pracoditā bhūtaiḥ śaṃsāsmai tāṃ priyām iti / na ca sābhyavadat sītāṃ pṛṣṭā rāmeṇa śocitā — wording differs).
- 3.64.9 रावणस्य च तत् रूपम् कर्माणि च दुरात्मनः | ध्यात्वा भयात् तु वैदेहीम् सा नदी न शशंस ह || (CE 3.60.9: … sā nadī
  na śaśaṃsa tām). She thought of Rāvaṇa's form and deeds, and out of fear she did not tell.
- 3.64.11ab एषा गोदावरी सौम्य किंचन् न प्रतिभाषते (Rāma: "this Godāvarī answers nothing"), vulgate only.

The deer (vulgate only; the site's markers close each verse after its second half):
- 3.64.15cd एते महा मृगा वीर माम् ईक्षन्ते पुनः पुनः | 3.64.16ab वक्तु कामा इह हि मे इंगितानि अनुपलक्षये ("these great
  deer keep looking at me; I see signs that they want to speak").
- 3.64.16cd तान् तु दृष्ट्वा नरव्याघ्र राघवः प्रत्युवाच ह | 3.64.17ab क्व सीत इति निरीक्षन् वै बाष्प संरुद्धया गिरा
  (Rāma asks them, his voice choked with tears: "Where is Sītā?").
- 3.64.17cd एवम् उक्ता नरेन्द्रेण ते मृगाः सहसा उत्थिता (asked by the king, the deer rose at once)
- **3.64.18** दक्षिण अभिमुखाः सर्वे दर्शयन्तो नभः स्थलम् | मैथिली ह्रियमाणा सा दिशम् याम् अभ्यपद्यत || (all facing
  south, showing the sky: the direction Maithilī took as she was carried off). This is the one whole numbered verse
  that fits a set piece.
- 3.64.19 तेन मार्गेण गच्छन्तो निरीक्षन्तो नराधिपम् | येन मार्गम् च भूमिम् च निरीक्षन्ते स्म ते मृगाः || (going that way,
  looking back at the king; the deer kept looking at the path and the ground)
- 3.64.20 पुनः नदन्तो गच्छन्ति लक्ष्मणेन उपलक्षिताः | तेषाम् वचन सर्वस्वम् लक्षयामास च इन्गितम् || (they go on, crying
  out; Lakṣmaṇa noticed them and read the whole meaning of their signs)
- 3.64.21–22: Lakṣmaṇa: "When you asked 'where is Sītā?', these deer rose at once and show the ground and the southern
  direction" (दर्शयन्ति क्षितिम् चैव दक्षिणाम् च दिशम् मृगाः); "let us go this way, to the south-west" (नैरृतीम्).
- 3.64.23cd बाढम् इति एव काकुत्स्थः प्रस्थितो दक्षिणाम् दिशम् (Rāma agrees and sets out south).
- IIT Kanpur (vedapath) numbers the deer line in its 3.64.17 and prints दक्षिणाभिमुखास्सर्वे दर्शयन्तो नभस्थलम्.
