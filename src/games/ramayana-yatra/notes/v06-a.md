## Paraśurāma (Bāla 1.75–76, Vālmīki only; Lakṣmaṇa does not speak)
Read on vr.net: 1.74.17–24 (his arrival; names), 1.75.1–4 and 25–28, 1.76.1–20. CE checked on the chapter pages 74, 75.
- **(a) the challenge: 1.75.3 `s-purayasva`** "draw this terrible great bow of Jamadagni's son full with an arrow and show
  your strength". Chosen over 1.75.27 (the line that names the bow वैष्णवं: vulgate तदेवं, CE तदिदं) and 1.75.28
  (vulgate शक्तोऽसि, CE शक्नोषि) because it is **identical in the vulgate and the CE**, uses easy words, and its verb
  पूरयस्व echoes पूरयामास of the bow verse. The context text says the bow is Viṣṇu's (1.75.11ff.).
- **(b) Rāma strings the bow: 1.76.5 `s-aropya`** "Rāma strung the bow, fitted the arrow; then, angry, Rāma said to Rāma
  Jāmadagnya…". The only verse that tells the stringing; teaches धनुः + रामः → धनू रामः. CE variant in pāda 4 only
  (वचः for इदम्), flagged. (1.76.4, the taking of the bow, also differs in the CE: प्रतिसंगृह्य.)
- **(c) the recognition: 1.76.17 `s-madhuhantaram`** "From your drawing of this bow I know you: the imperishable slayer of
  Madhu, lord of the gods. Blessings on you." Identical in the CE (1.75.17). MW confirms parāmarśa "drawing (of a bow)"
  and madhuhantṛ "N. of Rāma as an incarnation of Viṣṇu (R.)".
- Daśaratha's plea (1.75.5–8) is not a verse here; Phase 2 can narrate it.

## Decisions
- **Text form.** Bāla: the vr.net page letter for letter (it prints saṃhitā), orthography kept (मंत्र-, बभंज, कौसल्यानंद-,
  as संध्या in s-kausalya-supraja). Ayodhyā: vr.net prints word-split, so the joined pādas are the CE text letter for
  letter and vr.net confirms the words and the vulgate number (precedent: s-ramo-dvir, s-pitari-shushrusha,
  s-ramam-dasharatham). Every edition note says so.
- **Variants** (vulgate kept, the word carries `variant`, so it is never a recitation gap): वीर (s-krtartho-smi),
  मुदा (s-ahalya), इदम् (s-aropya), पालयतु + एनम् (s-gange).
- **1.30.26**: pādas 1–4 only (the speech; pādas 5–6 are narration). **1.67.19**: वर्जयित्वा (CE) for the page's typo.
- **1.73.26** chosen over 1.73.27 (its पतिव्रता … छायेवानुगता line is not in the CE; page typo महभागा).
- **Metre**: meterNotes on 6 pādas (vipulā): s-ahalya 3, s-aropayitva 1 (ma-vipulā), s-nipetush 3, s-iyam-sita 3,
  s-yan-mangalam 3 (na-vipulā), s-kshayanta 1 (bha-vipulā; the research said ma-vipulā, wrong). Text never edited.
- **Sandhi**: every half-verse rebuilds with the engine; no exceptions needed.
- **Lexicon**: ids already in lexicon-1/2 repeat lemma / pos / gender / meaning / notes / tags / provenance exactly and
  add only forms (same grammar string where the form already exists, so WP0's dedup merges the shlokaIds). New
  separate ids where the sense differs: `atibala-mantra` (not the adjective `atibala`), `bala-mantra`,
  `jamadagnya` (name) vs `jamadagnya-2` (adjective "Paraśurāma's", of the bow), `satyam` (adverb).
  `siddhashrama` has no gender (MW āśrama m. n.; the verse treats the name as neuter).
- **Names (names-3)**: person.janaka जनक, person.ahalya अहल्या, person.gautama गौतम, person.parashurama **जामदग्न्य**
  (Vālmīki calls him Jāmadagnya / Bhārgava / Rāma; परशु is only his axe, 1.74.19), place.mithila मिथिला, person.ganga गङ्गा.
- **सरलार्थः**: 2–4 sentences, ≤ 10 words each, every word ≤ L3 by the oracle (`levels.ts`), same register as the 21.

### The Mithilā told cutscene (painter "a"; source Bāla 1.49, 1.66–67, 1.73, 1.74–76; 75 steps)
1. road: Viśvāmitra leads the princes toward Mithilā's towers.
2. ashrama (Gautama's empty hermitage): **hidden hotspot "ahalya"** on the hut (7 s window). Found → Rāma steps in,
   Ahalyā appears, glow, her verse, petals (1.49.19). Passed by → the scene moves on (flag `found.mithila.ahalya` only when found).
3. sabha: Janaka (1.66.19, 26): "No king could even take hold of Shiva's bow. If Rama strings it, I will give him Sita."
   Rāma lifts the bow; **s-aropayitva**; flash + shake; the bow prop turns broken; **everyone falls but four** (Rāma,
   Lakṣmaṇa, Viśvāmitra, Janaka); **s-nipetush**; all rise.
4. wedding: Sītā before the fire facing Rāma (1.73.25); **s-iyam-sita**; petals (1.73.28).
5. storm (road home; Viśvāmitra has left, 1.74.1): darkness; Paraśurāma blocks the way; **s-purayasva**; Daśaratha
   pleads (1.75.6); Rāma takes and strings Viṣṇu's bow, **s-aropya**; "Parashurama's strength is gone" (1.76.11–12);
   **s-madhuhantaram**; the arrow (1.76.21); he bows and goes to Mahendra, the darkness lifts (1.76.22–24).
   Lakṣmaṇa never speaks (test).
Hands-off length: main path 125.8 s (76 s of it the six verses at the recitation pace), Ahalyā +22 s; every step can be
tapped through and the whole scene skipped (with a confirmation). Skipped or played, its main-path verses count as heard.
