## Decisions
- **Re-fetch method.** Every verse was re-fetched from its live valmikiramayan.net sarga page (WebFetch, asking for the
  raw lines around the verse marker, both lines of the couplet) and compared with the research text. All 15 match the
  research letter for letter (the research typo list is confirmed: गच्चेत्, इहान्त्तरे, निह्श्वसन्तीम्, विनश्येद्द्,
  आक्षसाः, भयन्, and the misprinted markers २-२२-१७, ९०-६-७१). The game stores corrected joined pādas.
- **5.1.132 settled: इहान्तरे** (vulgate: valmikiramayan.net with its doubled-त् typo corrected, and kasarabada.org);
  the critical edition reads इहान्तरा. Recorded in `edition` and provenance.
- **5.55.3 pāda 1: पुरुषश्रेष्ठा** (IIT Kanpur text; nom. pl. agreeing with ते / ये, and the sense of
  valmikiramayan.net's own translation "those great-souled men of excellence"). valmikiramayan.net and the CE print
  पुरुषश्रेष्ठ (only possible as a vocative); Sanskrit Wikisource reads धन्याः खलु महात्मानो. **Human reviewer decides.**
- **5.64.28:** valmikiramayan.net prints the couplet whole under ५-६४-२८ (IITK splits 28cd + 29ab); the first line
  (कौसल्या सुप्रजा राम समाश्वसिहि सुव्रत) is not in the critical edition (CE sarga 5.61 read in full: no कौसल्या /
  सुप्रजा / समाश्वसिहि); CE 5.61.16 pairs दृष्टा देवी… with a different second line.
- **6.60.54 fixed:** Sanskrit Wikisource (vulgate) confirms both halves (its 54cd + 55ab). CE reads तु for च in pāda 3.
- **Pāda cuts at a sandhi:** `s-tato-madhyat` pāda 3/4 महाशैलान् | मेरोरिव (as the CE print divides it);
  `s-dharmatma-satyasandhah` pāda 3 ends चाप्रतिद्वन्द्वस् (before तदेनं; precedent आयुष्मन्न् in s-aranya-3).
- **Spacing:** मध्यात् समुद्रस्य (vr.net and CE print the space); तव हेतोर्विशालाक्षि (all three witnesses print
  तवहेतोर्… joined; a word space is added, same letters); न पर्यदेवन्विधवा (vr.net, joined).
- **Metre:** all 60 pādas scan as pathyā except `s-na-paryadevan` pāda 1 (bha-vipulā, meterNote).
- **Edition variants flagged on the word** (`variant`, English; never a pūraṇa gap; verse on REVIEW-v06-c.md's
  human-review list): अन्तरे (s-tvarate-karyakalo), ते + पुरुषश्रेष्ठाः (s-dhanyas-te), च in pāda 3 (s-vadhyamano),
  तत् (s-dharmatma-satyasandhah, IITK शरैनं as reported by the research, not re-checked).
- **Roles:** gerundive स्थातव्यम् carries no role (precedent कर्तव्यम्, s-kausalya-supraja; with role "verb" it broke
  learning.test QA S1-01). Predicative past participles keep role "verb" (precedent निर्वृत्तम्).
- **Sandhi engine:** 14 verses rebuild; `s-tvarate-karyakalo` pādas 1–2 keep hiatus at the pāda break (मे | अहः) in all
  witnesses, where the engine gives मेऽहः → exception requested.
- **Grammar for the reviewer:** उदयन् (6.22.17) read as the thematic (epic) present participle of ud + √i (classical
  उद्यन्); पर्यदेवन् (6.128.99) is an active imperfect of pari + √dev (classical परिदेवते is middle); निःश्वसन्तीम् with
  -न्ती (thematic, epic). Every other finite verb / participle the oracle knows agrees with our label.
- **Lexicon:** MW could not be read this session (Cologne lookups are robots-blocked to the fetch tool; no network from
  scripts). New entries cite MW as the earlier packs did; their provenance notes say which are confirmed by the repo's
  own verified packs (@content/vocab `oracle:mw#…`/`gloss:mw`, @content/dhatu, @content/shabda: about 30 of 95) and
  which are asserted for a human spot-check. Five shared ids were aligned to package A's lexicon-3 (written first):
  dhimat "wise", krtartha (compound) "whose purpose is fulfilled", madhya "middle", rajya "kingdom, kingship", tatas
  "then; from there" (orchestrator's rule: for a new id, A's lemma/pos/meaning is the reference). 0 conflicts with
  lexicon-1…4 at 17:30 (v06-hooks test 18 green).
- **सरलार्थः:** 3–4 sentences each, ≤ 8 words a sentence, every word Level ≤ 3 by the oracle (checked per word).
