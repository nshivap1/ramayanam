## What the player does
- **Scene 7, the leap (Sundara 5.1).** The Kathā block of `ch5.leap_start` sets `katha.hanuman`: "You ride on his
  shoulder and guide his steps." From here the player walks as Hanumān (the student drawn on his shoulder). Surasā is a
  choice chain, no duel: Hanumān asks her to open her mouth; she opens it, he grows, twice; then "Grow larger again!"
  (she only opens wider and waits: back to the question) or "Become tiny!" (thumb-sized, in and out, her blessing).
  It can never be lost. Siṃhikā fights with `lead: hanuman` (every chapter-5 battle has it; only Kathā reads it).
- **Scene 8, Laṅkā by night (5.2–11).** Five P3 avoid-triggers on `lanka_city` (listed before the patrol triggers, so
  a lit tile is "seen" first; `once: false`; Kathā-only `requires`): the lamplit avenue (16–19 × 18–19, watched by a
  new Kathā sentry `guard_s` at 18,17), the palace gate's lamps (18–20 × 9–12, guard_a), the west lane (9 × 15–18,
  guard_b), the garden wall (32–34 × 15–18, guard_c). The view draws them as warm lamplight pools with a dotted rim.
  Stepping into one runs `ch5.seen_*`: "A guard turns toward the light! Hanumān slips back into the shadows." and a
  `warp` on the same map to the lane's mouth — no battle, no HP, no flag. The pointer, runGuide and the bots route
  around the light (findPath). In Kathā the patrol events set their flags with one line and no battle (guard_a /
  guard_c then vanish and their light goes out); talking to guard_b or guard_s: "The guard peers into the dark …".
- **Scene 9, the Aśoka grove (5.30–36).** Before showing himself Hanumān sings Rāma's story and the student sings with
  him: four short narration lines around three P1 recitations — `s-ramam-dasharatham:0` (the princes go to the forest),
  `s-golden-deer:1` (the deer), `s-aranya-3:3` (Rāvaṇa takes Sītā); a miss carries on.
- **Scene 10, Laṅkā ablaze (5.53–56).** After Akṣa (and the practice lines on Indrajit, the court, the tail set on fire)
  the Kathā block says the fire does not burn him (Sītā prayed to Agni), sets `ch5.ablaze` and warps to **`lanka_ablaze`**.
  Four roofs, one `roof_fire` tile each, in Vālmīki's order (5.54: Prahasta's house first, Kumbhakarṇa's among them,
  the mansions, Rāvaṇa's palace last); each roof's whole block bursts into flame (view: a warm flash, glow, embers) as
  Hanumān lands on it, in any order. After the fourth: "Only Vibhīṣaṇa's house is spared." / he dips his tail in the
  sea, fears for Sītā; the east gate (35,17) opens to the grove. Sītā is safe; she sends him home; `katha.hanuman` is
  unset; the leap home and the practice game's own homecoming lines at Kishkindhā; `ch5.complete`.

## Design decisions (deviations from the plan)
- **`lanka_ablaze` instead of `lanka_city`.** Tiles are static: a walkable `roof_fire` in `lanka_city` would be walkable
  and visible in the practice game too. `lanka_ablaze` is a copy of the city (same name `place.lanka_city`, signs,
  ground) with four `roof_fire` tiles, no NPCs, music `battle`; the only exit is the east gate (requires all four roofs,
  its lockedEvent repeats the objective). A script warp out of a roof event would strand logic-sim's `reach` (walkTo
  replans from the new map), hence the gate.
- The grove's west gate (0,11) has `requires: "!ch5.ablaze"` (lockedEvent `ch5.grove_stay`, Kathā): while Laṅkā burns
  the player cannot wander into the unburnt `lanka_city`. The practice game never sets the flag (hash unchanged).
- **Practice lines reused, not copied into Kathā blocks.** The end of Surasā's test and the Kishkindhā homecoming are the
  practice game's own lines, word for word, in blocks guarded by flags only Kathā sets (`ch5.surasa_tiny`,
  `ch5.ablaze`), not inside `if style.katha` — so they stay journey-neutral lines of sa-dialogue-3 and no Kathā line
  repeats a practice line.
- The Akṣa step expects `ch5.aksha_defeated` in both journeys; the roof steps and the Sītā step (expects `ch5.complete`)
  are `style: 'katha'`. Practice: Akṣa's script still ends the book at once (practice hash green).
- P4 rider: drawn in view-screen (`RIDER` masks, avatar a/b colours) beside Hanumān's head, on his right shoulder (behind
  the head in profile). Hanumān keeps the existing 'hanuman' sprite; the speech bubble of `person.hanuman` lines sits
  on the player while he walks as Hanumān.
- For WP-A (orchestrator's optional request): any figure on a water / shore tile is drawn in a wooden boat; figures side
  by side share one hull (Guha's ferry). The canvas mirrors the count on `data-boats`. (Only scripted moves put a
  figure on water; a future flying scene over water would get a boat too.)
