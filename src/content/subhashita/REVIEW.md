### 1b. Readings that differ from the commonly memorised form

- `prarabhyate-na-khalu-vighnabhayena`: Pañcatantra reading `विघ्नैः सहस्रगुणितैरपि हन्यमानाः प्रारब्धमुत्तमगुणा न परित्यजन्ति`
  (Nītiśataka: `विघ्नैः पुनः पुनरपि प्रतिहन्यमानाः प्रारब्धमुत्तमजना न परित्यजन्ति`).
- `shrotram-shrutenaiva-na-kundalena`: `विभाति कायः खलु सज्जनानां परोपकारेण न चन्दनेन` (Nītiśataka: `करुणापराणां परोपकारैर्न तु चन्दनेन`).
- `shaile-shaile-na-manikyam`: `साधवो नहि सर्वत्र`. · `hastasya-bhushanam-danam`: `भूषणे किं प्रयोजनम्` (often `भूषणैः`).
- `mata-shatruh-pita-vairi`: `येन बालो न पाठितः`. · `tyajed-ekam-kulasyarthe`: `आत्मार्थे पृथिवीं त्यजेत्` with hiatus after `°स्यार्थे`.
- `kashcit-kanta-viraha-guruna`: Kale's `स्वाधिकारात्प्रमत्तः`. · `eko-devah-keshavo-va-shivo-va`: with `ह्येकं`, `ह्येका`.
- `bg-17-16`: the corpus prints `मनः प्रसादः` as two words (usually the compound `मनःप्रसादः`).
- `bg-2-63`: `स्मृतिभ्रंशाद् बुद्धिनाशो` is printed with virāma and space in the corpus.
- Spelling follows each witness: सङ्गो (Gītā corpus) beside संपृक्तौ, परसंतापम्, शापेनास्तंगमित° (GRETIL).

### 1c. Attribution (`source`)

References follow the numbering of the e-text used, which may differ from the edition you know. Verses that occur
in several works are attributed to one of them (e.g. `अयं निजः परो वेति` → हितोपदेशः 1.71, also Pañcatantra 5.37;
`लालयेत्पञ्च वर्षाणि` → सुभाषितरत्नभाण्डागारम्, text identical in the Garuḍapurāṇa e-text).

### 1d. Pāda division of straddling words

Pādas are cut at akṣara boundaries, so a number of pādas begin with a conjunct that carries the end of the previous word
(`र्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि`, `स्तद्भागधेयं परमं पशूनाम्`, `र्दैवेन देयमिति …`). Confirm that this is acceptable for display, or
tell us the convention you prefer.

### 1f. Vipulā labels (19 verses)

The label is derived mechanically from syllables 5–7 of the odd pāda (na = ⏑⏑⏑, bha = –⏑⏑, ma = – – –, ra = –⏑–);
the further classical conditions on syllables 2–4 and the caesura are **not** checked.
